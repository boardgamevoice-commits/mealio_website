Safety & Privacy

AI you can trust in the kitchen.

Mealio is built with Safety-by-Design: every AI response prioritises your well-being over a pleasing answer.

🌡️

Doneness Safety Prompts

Recipes for poultry and pork always include internal temperature checkpoints and doneness cues. The AI will never skip food safety steps.

📵

Mandatory AI Disclaimers

Every visual doneness assessment includes a mandatory, non-dismissible safety reminder that AI cannot replace a meat thermometer.

🔒

Local-First Data

Your recipes and meal plans are stored exclusively on your device via CoreData. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Photos are sent only to Gemini API, and only temporarily.

🚫

No AI Recipe Edits Without You

The Copilot can suggest changes, but it will never silently modify your recipe steps or ingredients. Every update requires your explicit confirmation.

🩺

Non-Medical Nutrition Data

Calorie and macro estimates are clearly labelled as approximations and are not medical advice. Mealio is a cooking tool, not a health app.

⚠️

High-Risk Ingredient Detection

Prompts for raw meat, unpasteurised dairy, wild game, or other high-risk foods are intercepted and replaced with safe preparation guidance.
